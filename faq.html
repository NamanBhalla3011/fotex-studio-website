Frequently asked questions

Good things
to know.

Can’t find your answer? Send us a note. We’re happy to help.

How far in advance should we book?

For weddings, we recommend getting in touch 8–12 months ahead. Portrait and brand sessions can often be booked sooner.

Do you travel?

Absolutely. We are available for celebrations and projects throughout India and worldwide. Travel is quoted clearly in your proposal.

How many images will we receive?

It depends on your coverage, but a full wedding gallery generally includes 500–900 carefully edited images.

When will our gallery be ready?

We send a small preview within a week. Full wedding galleries are delivered within 8–10 weeks; portrait galleries within 3 weeks.

Do you offer films too?

Yes. We collaborate with trusted filmmakers and can include cinematic film coverage in a custom proposal.
